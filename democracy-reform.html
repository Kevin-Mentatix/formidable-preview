Democracy Reform & Policy Innovation

Start a conversation →How we think

Does Formidable recruit in democracy reform?

Yes. Democracy Reform & Policy Innovation is one of Formidable's six core sectors. The firm runs executive searches, team builds, fractional C-suite placements, and org design for organizations in this field.

Self-government is a craft, and like any craft it needs skilled people. Reform organizations, civic platforms, and policy shops need leaders who can grow a movement, run a disciplined organization, and earn trust across divides.

The leadership challenge

What makes hiring here different.

Growth is the mission

In civic work, reach is impact. Leaders need to grow members, coalitions, and adoption with the rigor of a growth-stage company.

Trust across lines

Nonpartisan work lives or dies on credibility. The right hire is respected by people who disagree with each other.

Campaign tempo, institution patience

Election cycles demand speed, but reform takes decades. Leaders have to work at both speeds.
