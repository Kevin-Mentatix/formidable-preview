What is team builds at Formidable?

Sometimes the need is not one person but a team: a new leadership bench after a raise, a function built from zero, or a spin-out that needs to be fully staffed. A team build treats those hires as one design problem.

Best for: Organizations after a funding round, new ventures and spin-outs, and family offices standing up an operating company.

How it works

What the work looks like.

  1. 01

    Design the team before the roles

    Who decides what, who reports to whom, and which skills must overlap. Getting this right first saves painful reorganizations later.

  2. 02

    Sequence the hires

    The order matters. The first leader you hire often helps recruit the next, so we plan the sequence on purpose.

  3. 03

    Hire for the whole

    Each candidate is evaluated against the team they will join, not only against a job description.

  4. 04

    Run it as one engagement

    One partner, one process, and one consistent candidate experience across every role.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is a team build?

A team build is a single engagement in which Formidable designs and recruits several related roles together, such as a full leadership team or a new department, instead of running separate searches.

Is a team build more efficient than separate searches?

Usually. Candidates see a coherent story about the team they are joining, and the design work is done once instead of role by role.

Building something meant to outlast you?

Tell us about the role, the team, or the organization you are trying to build. Every conversation starts with what you want the work to leave behind.
