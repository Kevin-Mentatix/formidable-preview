What is organizational design at Formidable?

Most hiring problems are structure problems in disguise. Org design looks at roles, reporting lines, and decision rights so the people you have, and the people you hire next, can do their best work.

Best for: Organizations from early stage to more than $100M in revenue that have outgrown their current structure.

How it works

What the work looks like.

  1. 01

    Map the work, not the org chart

    Start with what must get done and who should own it. Boxes and lines come last.

  2. 02

    Set clear decision rights

    Ambiguity about who decides is the most common source of friction in growing teams.

  3. 03

    Plan the next stage

    Design for the organization you are becoming, with a hiring roadmap to get there.

  4. 04

    Connect structure to hiring

    Every design produces a concrete list of roles, and Formidable can recruit them.

FAQ

Questions, answered

When does an organization need org design?

Common signals: decisions are slowing down, roles overlap, the founder is a bottleneck, or a new strategy needs a different team.

What experience does Formidable bring to org design?

Nathaniel Koloc has led org design and team building for organizations from early stage to more than $100M in revenue, and has run companies of up to about $25M in revenue as a general manager.

Building something meant to outlast you?

Tell us about the role, the team, or the organization you are trying to build. Every conversation starts with what you want the work to leave behind.
