What is executive search at Formidable?

A senior hire changes the trajectory of an organization. Formidable runs executive searches for founders, CEOs, and family offices who need a leader to carry real responsibility, and who want the search done by someone who understands the work.

Best for: Founders hiring their first executives, CEOs replacing or adding C-suite leaders, and family offices building operating teams.

How it works

What the work looks like.

  1. 01

    Scope the role around its legacy

    Before a single outreach, we define what the role must leave behind: the outcomes that will matter in five years, not just the bullet points that matter on day one.

  2. 02

    Search the people who are not looking

    The strongest candidates are usually busy doing great work. Reaching them takes relationships and a pitch that speaks to what they care about.

  3. 03

    Evaluate for mastery

    Track records are a start. We look for evidence of craft: how someone thinks, how they have grown, and how they make the people around them better.

  4. 04

    Close on freedom

    Great people choose where they work. We help you design an offer, from scope to compensation to flexibility, that a top candidate says yes to.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does an executive search take?

It depends on the seniority of the role and how specialized the profile is. Every search starts with a scoping conversation that sets a realistic timeline before outreach begins.

What roles does Formidable recruit for?

C-suite and senior leadership roles, including CEO, COO, CFO, Chief Growth Officer, executive director, and heads of functions, in the six sectors Formidable serves.

Who runs the search?

Searches are led by Nathaniel Koloc, who has run more than 50 executive searches over the past decade.

Building something meant to outlast you?

Tell us about the role, the team, or the organization you are trying to build. Every conversation starts with what you want the work to leave behind.
