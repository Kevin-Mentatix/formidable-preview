Manufacturing & Industrials

Start a conversation →How we think

Does Formidable recruit in manufacturing?

Yes. Manufacturing & Industrials is one of Formidable's six core sectors. The firm runs executive searches, team builds, fractional C-suite placements, and org design for organizations in this field.

A country that cannot make things cannot keep its promises. The new wave of industrial companies, from materials recyclers to advanced manufacturers, need operators who understand both the factory floor and the cap table.

The leadership challenge

What makes hiring here different.

Atoms are unforgiving

Software can be patched on Friday night. A production line cannot. Industrial leaders need judgment that only comes from having shipped physical product.

Startup speed, plant discipline

Hard-tech founders need executives who move like a startup and run safety, quality, and supply chain like a seasoned plant manager.

Scarce, specific talent

The people who have scaled a first-of-a-kind facility are a short list. Finding them takes relationships, not job boards.
