Does Formidable recruit in cozytech?

Yes. Cozytech is one of Formidable's six core sectors. The firm runs executive searches, team builds, fractional C-suite placements, and org design for organizations in this field.

Not every technology company should be optimized for attention. Cozytech is the growing set of products built around connection, belonging, and real life offline, and it needs leaders who can grow a business without turning users into a metric.

The leadership challenge

What makes hiring here different.

Growth with a conscience

The hardest job in cozytech is scaling without adopting the tactics the category exists to reject.

Product taste is strategy

In a category defined by how it feels, leaders need taste as much as they need dashboards.

Community is the moat

The best cozytech companies are built with their users, not on top of them. Leaders need to earn that relationship.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is cozytech?

Cozytech describes technology designed around human connection, belonging, and wellbeing: products meant to make life warmer and more social, rather than to maximize screen time.

What leadership roles does a cozytech company need first?

Usually a product or growth leader who can scale the user base while protecting the experience. Formidable often pairs that search with org design so the second and third hires are planned too.

Hiring in cozytech?

Tell us about the role and what it needs to accomplish. We will tell you honestly whether we are the right fit.
