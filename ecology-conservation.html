Ecology & Conservation

Protecting and restoring the living systems everything else depends on.

Start a conversation →How we think

Does Formidable recruit in ecology?

Yes. Ecology & Conservation is one of Formidable's six core sectors. The firm runs executive searches, team builds, fractional C-suite placements, and org design for organizations in this field.

Conservation works on the timescale of forests and watersheds. The organizations doing it need leaders who can raise durable capital, manage land and science, and build institutions that outlast any single campaign.

The leadership challenge

What makes hiring here different.

Generational stewardship

A land trust or restoration project is a promise to people who are not born yet. Leadership has to be built for that.

Science meets fundraising

Great conservation leaders translate ecology into a story donors, landowners, and agencies can act on.

Partnership-heavy work

Results come from coalitions of landowners, governments, tribes, and funders. The leader is the connector.
