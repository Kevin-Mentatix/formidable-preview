FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I start working with Formidable?

Email hello@formidable.services with a short note about your organization and the need. The first step is a conversation, not a contract.

Does Formidable work outside New York?

Yes. Formidable is based in Bedford, New York, and works with organizations across the United States.

What size organizations does Formidable work with?

From early-stage ventures to organizations with more than $100M in revenue, as well as family offices and the operating companies they back.

Does Formidable help job seekers find roles?

Formidable works on behalf of hiring organizations. Candidates whose experience fits one of the six sectors are welcome to get in touch.
