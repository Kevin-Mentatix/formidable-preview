Who is Nathaniel Koloc?

An executive recruiter and talent consultant who leads Formidable. He has led more than 50 executive searches, designed hiring systems that placed more than 12,000 people, co-founded the impact-careers company ReWork, and writes about hiring and meaningful work.

Nathaniel has spent his career at the intersection of talent and purpose: helping people find meaningful work, and helping mission-driven organizations find the people who can carry it.

In 2011 he co-founded ReWork with Evan Walden, as part of the Unreasonable Institute. ReWork connected impact-driven professionals with organizations creating measurable social and environmental impact, worked with more than 100 such organizations, and was covered by Harvard Business Review, Fast Company, and Forbes. In January 2018 it was acquired by Koya Leadership Partners.

Since then he has worked as an executive recruiting partner, operator, and advisor. He has run companies of up to about $25M in revenue as a general manager, led org design from early stage to more than $100M in revenue, and been a principal on both the buy and sell side of M&A.
